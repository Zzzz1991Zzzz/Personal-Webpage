\theme{RL for LLM reasoning}
\project{What rollout tokens buy in RLVR}{Under review, ICLR 2027}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Studied GRPO on competition mathematics with SmolLM3-3B (full-parameter, verl) and Qwen3.5-9B/35B-A3B (LoRA), scoring every model by majority-vote accuracy under a fixed budget of generated tokens per problem.
  \item Replacing one third of SmolLM3-3B's training problems with harder ones doubled response length (+101\%) on the unchanged 2,048 problems at a similar training reward.
  \item Halving Qwen3.5-9B's training response limit from 4,096 to 2,048 tokens cut training compute by 28\% and raised majority-vote accuracy at 16,384 tokens per problem from 36.1\% to 43.5\%.
  \item Showed that coverage and majority vote can rank a base model and its RL-trained version in opposite orders (57 vs.\ 39 problems with any correct sample; 5 vs.\ 18 correct majority answers).
\end{itemize}
\project{QbQ: self-evolving curriculum for RL fine-tuning}{arXiv 2026, under review at AAAI 2027; co-first author}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Co-developed Question-begets-Question (QbQ): a teacher rewrites existing problems into diverse variants, and each round seeds new problems from those the current checkpoint can mostly solve ($8 \le$ pass@16 $\le 15$).
  \item Trained Qwen2.5-Math-7B with GRPO on problem statements and final answers only, never on teacher reasoning traces; under the same 6,000-problem budget the curriculum lifted AIME pass@1 from 5.6\% to 16.5\%, past the 12.5--14.5\% ceiling of static augmentation, with no saturation after 20 rounds.
\end{itemize}

\theme{Benchmarks and AI for science}
\project{CT Open: live, decontaminated clinical-trial forecasting}{COLM 2026}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Contributed to a live platform that runs four challenges a year and scores only predictions submitted before a trial's results became public; released a training set and two time-stamped test benchmarks (Winter 2025 and Summer 2025).
  \item Contributed to the automated decontamination pipeline (temporal filters, iterative LLM web search, expert review); a manual audit found no pre-cutoff results for 49 of 50 sampled trials.
\end{itemize}
\project{DeepImagine: counterfactual imagination for clinical-trial prediction}{Under review, ICLR 2027}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Predicts a target trial's outcome from a historical trial by changing one differing factor at a time (enrollment, study arm, outcome measure); with seven anchors, lifts GPT-5 from 72.4 (RAG) to 79.0 macro-F1 on 701 CT Open questions.
  \item Contributed to per-factor Gemma-3-12B specialists trained on natural counterfactuals from 14,000 trials with GPT-5 reasoning traces; the trained system reaches 78.7 macro-F1, above all ten conventional and clinical predictors.
\end{itemize}

\theme{Interpretability}
\project{Latent Assembly: executable operations in language models}{Under review, ICLR 2027}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Contributed to a causal framework that recovers typed program states and state-dependent operations from unlabeled activations, validated on compiled transformers and on SELECT and LOAD operations in Qwen, Gemma and Llama.
  \item LOAD, ADD and COMPARE operations discovered independently on separate tasks in Qwen2.5-14B link into a chain that executes a held-out program at 84\% accuracy vs.\ 49\% zero-shot, with no target-task fitting.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{3pt}
\entry{Efficient LLM serving (collaboration with Harvard University)}{2026}
      {LoopKV: loop-aware KV cache compression for looped transformers}{arXiv 2026; co-first author}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Characterized Ouro's per-loop KV cache: quantization sensitivity varies per (loop, layer) cell and adds up to the output KL, later loops tolerate sharing the final loop's KV, and read errors compound across loops (4.7$\times$ less damage at equal bytes when low-attention values are read at 2 bits rather than skipped).
  \item Co-designed an offline profiler and knapsack planner that assign INT4, INT2 or KV sharing to each cell, attention-aware V precision at decode time, and one fused vLLM kernel that executes every cell type in a single launch (host launches per decode step from 504 to 17 with CUDA graphs).
  \item On Ouro-1.4B (one RTX PRO 6000), LoopKV-M stores 35\% less KV than uniform INT4 (KIVI-4) and sustains 1.7--2.2$\times$ its goodput under a 16K latency SLO; 1.13--4.44$\times$ BF16 decode throughput on three looped models with no suite average significantly below BF16.
\end{itemize}
